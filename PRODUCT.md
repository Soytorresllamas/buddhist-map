# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Any curious person drawn to contemplative philosophy or Buddhist thought — no prior knowledge required. They may arrive from a WhatsApp share, a social post, or a personal invitation. They explore at their own pace: some scan the map for a few minutes, others play the game, others go deep into the guide. Context: quiet, personal, likely evening or weekend exploration on a laptop or phone.

## Product Purpose

An interactive Buddhist wisdom map that makes Dharma concepts approachable, interconnected, and memorable. Three surfaces: a spatial concept map (index.html), a canvas game where a monk walks the Eightfold Path (el-camino-del-monje.html), and a full-featured interactive guide (octuple-noble-sendero.html). Success looks like a visitor leaving with one idea that stays with them — a concept they'd want to share or revisit.

## Brand Personality

Contemplative · Illuminated · Serene grandeur

The aesthetic is earned — dark ground, gold light, premium serif typography. It feels like a manuscript that became interactive, not an app that added Buddhist themes. Voice is warm and precise; neither academic nor casual.

## Anti-references

- Cold data dashboards (functional, soulless, clinical color palettes)
- Meditation-app minimalism (Calm, Headspace — pastel bubbles, soft sans-serifs, wellness-consumer tone)
- Institutional religion sites (rigid, formal, clip-art iconography)
- Academic wikis (dense text, no visual personality)

## Design Principles

1. **Illuminate, don't lecture** — spatial relationships and interaction reveal meaning before text explains it.
2. **Serene grandeur** — the darkness carries weight; gold is used sparingly and earns its glow.
3. **Depth without overwhelm** — every surface has layers, but none force the visitor deeper than they want to go.
4. **Motion as ceremony** — animation should feel like a ritual gesture: deliberate, purposeful, never decorative.
5. **Discovered, not delivered** — the best moments (the hearts, the Bodhi tree, the connections) are found by the user, not announced.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Critical requirements: `prefers-reduced-motion` honored on all animations (the canvas game included), contrast ≥ 4.5:1 on body text against dark backgrounds, keyboard navigation on the concept map. The canvas game is by nature less accessible; provide text-alternative descriptions where possible.
